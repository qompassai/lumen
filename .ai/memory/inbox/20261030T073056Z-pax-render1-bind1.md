# RENDER-1 + BIND-1 landed on pax/lumen-render1 (base origin main 16e4cfc)

## What

- **BIND-1 (R-614)**: optional `translation_keyforms` lattice on Part
  (`TranslationForm { at, translation: [f32; 2] }`, absolute model-space
  translations, §3.7 lattice law), admitted as a recorded amendment at
  `format_version: 1` — a v2 bump was rejected because
  `crates/fixtures/adversarial/bad-version.json` is itself version 2 and
  an existing test expects it refused with LRF-106; SPEC §10 records the
  exception and states it is not precedent (the next new field bumps).
  Absent field = byte-identical emission (`skip_serializing_if`).
- **rig-core evaluation**: `pose.rs` (lattice sampling, deformer chains
  root-first per §3.4, rotation `origin + R·S·(p − origin)`, warp
  inverse-bilinear with edge extension, part translations summed down
  the part chain and applied after the deformer chain, effective
  opacity = mesh × Π parts), `physics.rs` (SPEC §5 Verlet sim exactly;
  reading pinned: simulated particle 0 is the anchor, the strand's
  listed particles are the free particles), `runtime.rs` (`Rig`).
- **RENDER-1 (R-615)**: `render.rs` CPU rasteriser (draw sort
  `(draw_order, id)`, barycentric coverage, bilinear texture sampling
  with v top-first, all 18 W3C blend modes, one-level clipping masks,
  f32 framebuffer with single quantisation) + `examples/render_frame.rs`
  batch CLI (camera fit at rest and held). Missing textures fail loudly
  (`RenderError::MissingTexture`), never substituted.
- **Bridge** (torogao `pax/bind1-bridge` at f61e094 base): rigexport
  `build_part_translation` converts node `transform.t.x/t.y` bindings
  into part lattices via the walk's `delta_to_world` (the same
  conversion mesh deform offsets use); retired drop reason updated.
  Aka: 57/100 parts gain lattices, total drops 193 → 98, transform.t
  drops 126 → 31. Aka's brow bindings span 3 axes (Yaw-Pitch x/y +
  Blink) and still drop — the format's 2-axis lattice law names the
  limit. Aka's export (76 textures) still refuses at the pre-existing
  TEXTURES_MAX 64 cap on this base; the 64→256 alignment lives on the
  moc3 landing branch (aee0a96) and composes at landing.

## Named gaps (SPEC §3.2 / module docs)

- Part translations do not move deformer origins or physics anchors.
- INP local-frame rotation of translation deltas under animated
  ancestors is not reproduced; converters bake rest-frame vectors.
- INP RK4 rigid/spring pendulum trajectories, spring rest-angle
  forces, length/frequency offsets, per-stage provider re-reads, and
  its per-triangle affine warp lattice have no §5/§3.4 counterpart and
  are not approximated.
